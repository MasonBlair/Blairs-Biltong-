What actually is biltong?

Air-dried beef, seasoned with vinegar and spices, hung to dry slowly rather than cooked. It's a South African thing that's been made the same way for a couple of hundred years.

How is it different from jerky?

Jerky is usually cooked or smoked at heat, sliced thin, and often sweet. Biltong is cured with vinegar and spice, then air dried over days at low temperature. The result is softer, beefier, and less sweet — it tastes like beef rather than like marinade.

What's the difference between Original and Dry Heat?

Original is the traditional recipe — salt, pepper, coriander seed. Classic, and where we'd start you.

Dry Heat is the same base with chilli through it. It's a slow, dry heat that builds rather than a sharp hit up front.

Blair — worth adding: how hot is Dry Heat really? Something like "about as hot as a decent chorizo" gives people a reference point they understand.
