Our story

Made for the mountains

[Placeholder copy — replace with the real story.]

Blair's Biltong started in a Queenstown kitchen, with a family recipe carried over from South Africa and a stubborn belief that good biltong can't be rushed. No shortcuts, no fillers, no ingredients you can't pronounce.

Every batch is hand seasoned and air dried for days — the way it's always been done. It's the snack we take up the hill, into the truck, and out on the water. We think you'll want it there too.
