Shipping

We hand pack every order in Queenstown and send it out as quickly as we can.

What it costs

New Zealand$5.00 flat rate, tracked
Orders over $50Free shipping
OverseasNot at this stage — New Zealand only

How long it takes

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 business days of your order
  • Delivery: usually 2–5 business days after dispatch
  • Rural addresses can take an extra day or two

Orders placed over a weekend or public holiday go out on the next working day. We'll email you tracking once your parcel is on its way.

Buying in person

If you're local and picking up from us directly, use your free shipping code at checkout and the delivery charge comes off. You'll still get a proper receipt, and we'll arrange the handover with you.

Something not right?

If your order hasn't turned up when you expected it, or it arrives damaged, email sales@blairsbiltong.co.nz and we'll sort it. See our returns page for how that works.
